# Image: Before / After

**User request (vague, one line):**

> "I need a cover for my book about staying calm as a founder while your
> company is on fire."

**prompt-king output** (following the `OUTPUT MODE` contract in
[`SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md)):

---

**1. Selected visual type:** `BOOK_FRONT_COVER` (Books / Publishing group)

**2. Purpose:** Retail and Amazon-thumbnail-ready cover for a business
nonfiction title on founder resilience — needs to read clearly at thumbnail
size and signal "credible, calm authority," not generic startup-hustle
imagery.

**3. Selected commands:**
`BOOK_FRONT_COVER` + `EDITORIAL_TYPOGRAPHY` + `LUXURY_SERIF` + `MINIMAL_TEXT`
+ `SWISS_TYPE` grid discipline (Typography Directives) — restrained
minimalist composition (Design Principles) — muted, controlled color
(Color Art Direction), no literal "fire" cliché (Anti-AI / Anti-Generic
Directives: avoid literal metaphor).

**4. Final production prompt:**

> Create a book front cover for "The Quiet Edge: How Calm Founders Win."
>
> Subject: A single lit match held steady and upright against a dark navy
> background, flame small and controlled rather than raging — the calm
> center, not the chaos around it.
>
> Visual language: Minimalist editorial book cover, restrained luxury
> nonfiction aesthetic, muted navy-and-warm-ember palette, no gradient
> effects, no stock-photo business imagery.
>
> Composition: Centered match, generous negative space above and below,
> Swiss-grid alignment for title block in the lower third.
>
> Camera: Straight-on macro product-style framing, shallow depth of field,
> flame in sharp focus.
>
> Lighting: Single controlled key light on the flame, background falls to
> near-black — high contrast, low ambient fill.
>
> Materials: Matte paper-stock texture implied in the render, not glossy.
>
> Typography: Large display luxury serif for the title, small supporting
> sans for the subtitle and author name, generous letter-spacing, no
> decorative flourishes.
>
> Environment: Void/negative space, no set dressing.
>
> Realism: Photoreal render of the match and flame; typography rendered
> crisp and legible, not distorted.
>
> Mood: Controlled, quiet, confident — the opposite of startup-hustle
> chaos.
>
> Aspect ratio: 2:3 (Book Cover, per Format Directives)
>
> Avoid: literal burning buildings, flame filling the frame, orange/red
> gradient clichés, stock "stressed founder at laptop" imagery, generic
> sans-serif business-book type.

**5. Aspect ratio:** 2:3

**6. Avoid list:** literal fire/disaster imagery, gradient-heavy startup
clichés, cluttered cover text, generic corporate stock-photo look.
