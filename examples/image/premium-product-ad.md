# Example: Premium Product Advertisement Prompt

This example demonstrates how Prompt King transforms a simple user request for a premium product ad into a highly detailed visual production prompt.

## User Request
> "Create a premium ad visual for our new luxury watches. Something clean and expensive looking."

---

## Compiled Prompt King Output

### 1. SELECTED VISUAL TYPE
Commercial Product Photography — Luxury Watch Hero Shot (`PRODUCT_HERO` + `COMMERCIAL_REALISM`)

### 2. PURPOSE
High-impact product visual for luxury watch marketing, designed to convey high value, precise craftsmanship, and exclusive branding.

### 3. SELECTED COMMANDS
`PRODUCT_HERO`, `COMMERCIAL_REALISM`, `EYE_LEVEL`, `STUDIO_SOFTBOX`, `METAL_TEXTURE`, `GLASS_TEXTURE`, `CENTERED_COMPOSITION`, `NEGATIVE_SPACE`, `1:1`

### 4. FINAL PRODUCTION PROMPT

```text
Create a commercial product photograph of a luxury watch for marketing.

Subject:
An elegant automatic wristwatch with a polished stainless steel casing and a dark slate gray brushed metal dial. Detailed view of the hands pointing to 10:10, showing the micro-textures of the dial, the polished bevels of the indices, and the reflection on the sapphire crystal glass.

Visual language:
Minimalist layout with a clean commercial aesthetic, focusing on the tactile quality of physical metal and glass materials.

Composition:
Centered composition with generous negative space around the watch to leave ample room for typography and brand messaging.

Camera:
Eye-level perspective, shot on a 90mm macro lens, f/8 aperture to maintain front-to-back sharpness of the watch face.

Lighting:
Studio softbox lighting from the left to highlight the polished steel edges, with a soft diffused fill light from the right to lift shadow density and reveal metal brushed textures without introducing harsh glares.

Materials:
Polished steel, brushed slate gray metal, sapphire crystal glass refraction, micro-engraved dial indices, clean matte surface underneath.

Environment:
Seamless infinite dark charcoal gray background with a soft, diffused contact shadow directly beneath the watch for physical grounding.

Realism:
Commercial realism, realistic metallic reflections, physically plausible caustics, and subtle real-world surface imperfections (no plastic default AI gloss).

Mood:
Sophisticated, pristine, exclusive.

Aspect ratio:
1:1
```

### 5. ASPECT RATIO
1:1 (Square)

### 6. OPTIONAL NEGATIVE / AVOID LIST
*Generated via Negative Prompt Intelligence Engine (Negative base: `NEG-V1`)*
```text
Avoid: harsh shadows, busy background, blown-out highlights, generic AI art look, default digital art style, glossy render look, stock photo look, lens flare overload, bokeh overload, airbrushed look, over-saturated colors, floating objects, cropped subject, cut-off edges, warped anatomy, distorted watch hands, crooked dial indices.
```
