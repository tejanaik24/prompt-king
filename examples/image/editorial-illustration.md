# Example: Editorial Illustration Prompt

This example shows how Prompt King translates a conceptual request into a structured illustration brief suited for premium print or web editorial layouts.

## User Request
> "Need an illustration for an article about the rise of remote work in dense cities."

---

## Compiled Prompt King Output

### 1. SELECTED VISUAL TYPE
Editorial Illustration — Conceptual Urban Landscape (`EDITORIAL_COLLAGE` + `MAGAZINE_TYPE`)

### 2. PURPOSE
Editorial feature illustration for a magazine or high-end news publication, communicating the concept of isolation and home offices inside a dense urban framework.

### 3. SELECTED COMMANDS
`EDITORIAL_COLLAGE`, `PAINTERLY_EDITORIAL`, `RULE_OF_THIRDS`, `MATTE_PAPER`, `SWISS_TYPE`, `REAL_PAPER_TEXTURE`, `4:5`

### 4. FINAL PRODUCTION PROMPT

```text
Create an editorial feature illustration for a magazine layout.

Subject:
A towering, dense urban apartment complex made of modular concrete blocks. One single apartment window glow with a warm, inviting amber light showing a silhouette of a person working at a desk, while all other windows remain dark and cold in shades of steel blue and gray.

Visual language:
Modern editorial illustration, painterly textures, visible gouache brush strokes, minimalist color palette with high contrast between the warm single window and the cool building facade.

Composition:
Rule of thirds, asymmetrical composition with the glowing window placed on the lower-right intersection line. Massive negative space of the dark building facade on the left.

Camera:
Flat graphic projection, medium-telephoto lens perspective to compress the architectural layers.

Lighting:
Warm incandescent key light coming from the single interior window, contrasting against cold twilight blue ambient lighting on the concrete exterior.

Materials:
Matte paper texture, dry brush strokes on the building edges, subtle canvas fiber pattern, screen-print lithograph texture.

Environment:
A quiet urban cityscape in the background recedes into flat geometric shapes at twilight.

Realism:
Hand-made human appearance, subtle organic alignment flaws in printing layers, non-uniform paper grain, no generic smooth 3D surfaces.

Mood:
Quiet, contemplative, isolated, focused.

Aspect ratio:
4:5
```

### 5. ASPECT RATIO
4:5 (Standard vertical portrait layout for magazine print)

### 6. OPTIONAL NEGATIVE / AVOID LIST
*Generated via Negative Prompt Intelligence Engine (Negative base: `NEG-V1`)*
```text
Avoid: generic AI art look, default digital art style, glossy render look, 3D CGI rendering, plastic skin, airbrushed textures, lens flare overload, over-saturated neon colors, perfectly symmetrical composition, floating objects, cluttered background.
```
