# Example: Cinematic Video Prompt

This example demonstrates Prompt King's compilation of a cinematic video prompt, complete with temporal motion pacing, shot framing, and camera movement taxonomy.

## User Request
> "A cinematic video of an explorer walking through ancient ruins in a jungle."

---

## Compiled Prompt King Output

### 1. SELECTED VISUAL TYPE
Cinematic Video — High-Fidelity Adventure Film Still (`CINEMATIC_FILM_STILL` + `EPIC_CINEMATIC`)

### 2. PURPOSE
High-impact cinematic shot for an adventure/fantasy film sequence or promotional reel.

### 3. SELECTED COMMANDS
`CINEMATIC_FILM_STILL`, `EPIC_CINEMATIC`, `NATURAL_LIGHT_CINEMA`, `DOLLY_IN`, `SLOW_MOTION`, `CONTINUOUS_MOVEMENT`, `16:9`

### 4. FINAL PRODUCTION PROMPT

```text
Create a cinematic video shot for an adventure film sequence.

Subject:
A lone explorer in a dusty canvas jacket and cargo trousers, carrying a leather backpack, slowly walking away from the camera through a crumbling stone archway covered in thick jungle vines.

Visual language:
Epic cinematic film still, documentary realism, 35mm film grain, desaturated organic color palette.

Composition:
Wide shot, central composition with the archway framing the explorer. Towering ancient trees and hanging vines frame the foreground.

Camera Movement:
Dolly-in camera motion, moving slowly and smoothly forward tracking behind the explorer at eye level.

Action & Pacing:
Slow-motion walking speed, dust particles floating lazily in the light shafts, leaves swaying gently in the background breeze. Continuous, stable camera tracking.

Lighting:
Volumetric sunbeams (god rays) filtering through the dense jungle canopy, illuminating the dust and lighting the explorer's path; soft rim light on shoulders.

Materials:
Weathered moss-covered stone textures, coarse canvas fabric, rough tree bark, damp soil.

Environment:
Deep, humid jungle containing ancient Mayan-style stone ruins, overgrown with ferns and climbing ivy.

Duration:
8 seconds

Aspect ratio:
16:9
```

### 5. ASPECT RATIO
16:9 (Landscape standard)
