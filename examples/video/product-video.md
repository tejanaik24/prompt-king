# Example: Product Video Prompt

This example demonstrates how Prompt King builds product video prompts for social media ad placements with smooth macro pan movements.

## User Request
> "5-second Instagram Reel ad video showing perfume liquid splashing out of the bottle."

---

## Compiled Prompt King Output

### 1. SELECTED VISUAL TYPE
Product Advertisement Video — High-Speed Commercial Splash Shot (`PRODUCT_HERO` + `BEAUTY_EDITORIAL`)

### 2. PURPOSE
5-second social media reel ad for a luxury fragrance brand, designed to emphasize perfume liquid texture, bottle reflection, and dynamic motion.

### 3. SELECTED COMMANDS
`PRODUCT_HERO`, `BEAUTY_EDITORIAL`, `CLOSEUP`, `SLOW_PAN_LEFT`, `SLOW_MOTION` (high speed), `GLASS_TEXTURE`, `9:16`

### 4. FINAL PRODUCTION PROMPT

```text
Create a commercial product video for a luxury perfume advertisement.

Subject:
A heavy cylindrical glass perfume bottle with a polished silver cap. A stream of golden perfume liquid splashes elegantly from the nozzle, forming beautiful micro-droplets frozen in mid-air.

Visual language:
Beauty editorial, commercial product photography, crisp clarity, luxury neutral colors.

Composition:
Closeup shot, centered composition, focusing on the spray nozzle and the splashing liquid.

Camera Movement:
Slow pan-left motion, tracking smoothly around the curved glass surface of the bottle at eye level.

Action & Pacing:
Ultra slow-motion (high-speed camera style), the liquid splash spreads slowly and gracefully, with droplets catching crisp specular light reflections.

Lighting:
Studio softbox lighting from the side, producing soft-edged highlights on the glass and brilliant reflections on the silver cap. Clean fill lighting to maintain shadow detail.

Materials:
Heavy clear glass refraction, metallic chrome finish, viscous golden liquid droplets.

Environment:
Seamless matte beige studio backdrop, clean and dry surface with a soft contact shadow underneath the bottle.

Duration:
5 seconds

Aspect ratio:
9:16
```

### 5. ASPECT RATIO
9:16 (Instagram Reel / Story vertical format)

### 6. OPTIONAL NEGATIVE / AVOID LIST
*Generated via Negative Prompt Intelligence Engine (Negative base: `NEG-V1`)*
```text
Avoid: quick camera shakes, blurred focus, cheap plastic sheen, garbled text on bottle label, warped bottle geometry, harsh glare, over-saturated colors, flat lighting, floating bottles, cropped bottle edges, static image.
```
