# Video: Before / After

**User request (vague, one line):**

> "Make a video for my new vitamin C serum, it's for Instagram."

**prompt-king output** (following the reasoning chain in
[`SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md): USER INTENT → VIDEO OBJECTIVE → FORMAT →
STORY/SHOT STRUCTURE → CAMERA → SUBJECT MOTION → ENVIRONMENT → LIGHTING →
AUDIO → CONTINUITY → PLATFORM OPTIMIZATION → FINAL VIDEO PROMPT):

---

**1. Selected visual type:** `PRODUCT_VIDEO` / `REEL` (Production Categories)

**2. Purpose:** 8-second Instagram Reel hook that sells "brightening,
clinical-grade skincare" through texture and light, not a talking-head ad.

**3. Selected commands:** `PRODUCT_HERO` framing (image library, Product /
Commercial group) as the opening beat, `DOLLY_IN` camera move (Camera
Movement), first-frame hook via motion-into-frame (First-Frame / Hook
Strategy), `PRODUCT_MACRO` texture beat (Product / Commercial group,
cross-referenced), `AMBIENT_BED` + `FOLEY_HIT` audio (Sound Design
Direction).

**4. Final production prompt** (Veo 3 style — flowing paragraph per
`reference/platform-cheatsheets.md`):

> A single amber glass serum bottle sits centered on a wet, glossy black
> surface, backlit by a soft golden key light. The camera dollies in
> slowly from a medium shot to a tight hero shot as a single drop of
> clear-gold liquid falls from an eyedropper into frame, hitting the
> surface below the bottle and rippling outward in slow motion. Cut to an
> extreme close-up macro shot of the serum's texture on skin — light
> catching individual droplets, shallow depth of field, warm practical
> lighting. Ambient soundscape: quiet studio room tone with a single crisp
> foley hit on droplet impact. No dialogue. End on the bottle back in
> focus with the brand mark clean and legible.
>
> Aspect ratio: 9:16 (Instagram Reel, per Format Directives). Duration: 8
> seconds (Veo 3.1 single-generation cap, per platform cheat-sheet).
> Frame rate: 24fps for the macro beat to read as premium, not 30fps
> social-standard throughout.
>
> Avoid: static unclaimed opening frame, cluttered background props,
> harsh clinical white lighting, on-screen text walls, generic stock
> "influencer holding product" framing.

**5. Format:** 9:16, 8 seconds, 24fps

**6. Avoid list:** static open, clinical white lighting, on-screen text
clutter, generic influencer-hand-holding-product shot.
